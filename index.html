The Art of
Designing Sound

People design sound? Yes.

Sound designers should get more recognition.

The art of sound should be appreciated by all,
and by all I mean those who watch films, tv series, or play video games.

What is sound design you may ask? It can be a multitude of things:

  1. It creates an auditory experience for film, tv, games, and more.
  2. It crafts and manipulates sound elements such as ambience, music, and sound effects.
  3. It invokes certain emotions, immerses, and directs attention to the audience.

Sound designers take an immense amount of time to create the experiences you love.

It isn't just choosing sound effects and being done, it's about creating some from scratch.
Some people literally go out into the wilderness to collect bear noises.

It isn't just putting everything together into a mixing software and calling it a day.
Attributes have to be added to each sound element such as noise cancellation, EQ, compression, etc.
This is the time consuming part. I can confidently say that this part sometimes puts people over the edge.

Anyways, that's all that needs to be said.
Do your research and become more aware of the sounds around you.
They were lovingly crafted.
